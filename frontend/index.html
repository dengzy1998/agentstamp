What is this?

Agent ID is Binance Wallet's on-chain identity for AI agents — a non-transferable ERC-721 token on BNB Chain. If your wallet holds one, it proves you registered an agent identity (yours looks like yourname.ai).

AgentStamp lets Agent ID holders check in once a day. Keep your streak alive and earn soulbound badge NFTs at 3, 7, 14, 21 and 30 total check-ins. Badges can never be transferred or sold — they only prove you showed up.

⚠️ Please read

Each check-in is a real BNB Chain transaction and costs a small amount of BNB in gas (usually a few cents). Badges are collectible proof-of-attendance tokens with no promised value — they cannot be transferred or sold. AgentStamp's contract has no owner or admin keys and never touches your funds; it only records check-ins and mints badges.
